📖 About the Show

Fruits Basket is an anime based on a manga by Natsuki Takaya. It follows Tohru Honda, an orphaned high school girl who ends up living with the Sohma family. The Sohmas are cursed: whenever they are hugged by someone of the opposite sex, they transform into an animal of the Chinese zodiac.
